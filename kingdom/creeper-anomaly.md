# 💥 Creeper Anomaly

A mysterious artifact has fallen from the sky into the desert close to Prime City. It radiates an unstable aura, causing frequent lightning strikes in the night and drawing a massive swarm of creepers to the area. They have slowly pushed toward the city walls and managed to destroy a section of them. Sir Jameson has posted a reward for any brave heroes willing to contain the threat. ⚡

## ⚠️ Survival Tips

- **Night Terrors:** When night falls, frequent lightning strikes will naturally charge the creepers. Charged creepers deal massive explosion damage and can easily one-shot under-armored players.
- **Watch the Sky:** Lightnings are incredibly common during the night storm and can strike players directly. If your gear is low-tier, stick to daytime hunting and find high ground or evacuate before dusk.
- **Recommended Gear:** Bring gear with **Blast Protection** and a sword with **Knockback** or a bow to keep them out of their explosion fuse radius.
